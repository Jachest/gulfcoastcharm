Questions

Good to know before you book.

Is the volleyball court really private?
Yes. It sits in the fenced backyard and is reserved for your group for the length of your stay. We rake it before you arrive and keep balls on the rack. Everyone who plays signs a short recreation waiver, which we send with your booking confirmation.
How far is the beach, honestly?
About four minutes by car to St. Pete Beach. Beach chairs, umbrellas and a cooler are in the house, so you can be on the sand with everything you need fifteen minutes after you decide to go.
Can we bring our dog?
Pets are welcome with a pet fee, and there is a designated relief area outside. Let us know how many when you request your dates.
Can we swim or kayak from the dock?
The dock is for coffee, cocktails and watching the herons. Bear Creek is tidal and the dock edge is view-only, with no water entry. Shell Key and Fort De Soto are the spots for paddling, and we are happy to point you to rentals.
What are the quiet hours?
We are in a residential neighborhood and we keep good relationships with our neighbors. Quiet hours are in the house guide, and we ask that games and music wind down in the evening. Groups that respect this are welcome back any time.
What does a stay cost, all in?
Nightly rates start around $263 Sunday through Thursday and $309 on Friday and Saturday, with higher rates in peak season and on holiday weekends. Every booking adds a cleaning fee, a $79 damage protection fee that covers accidental damage so we never ask for a large deposit, Florida and Pinellas County lodging taxes, and a pet fee if you bring one. The booking calendar shows the exact total for your dates before you pay anything. For larger groups and celebrations we may place a refundable hold on a card before arrival; it is released after checkout.
Why book direct instead of on a platform?
Platforms now charge a service fee of roughly fifteen percent on every stay. Booking here means that money stays in your budget, you deal with us directly, and you can add court time, beach setups and keepsakes that platforms cannot offer.
